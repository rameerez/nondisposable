# Privacy relays: forwarding addresses in front of a REAL, long-lived inbox.
#
# They are not throwaways (the person behind one gets your email for as long as
# they keep the alias), but they do let one person hold many addresses. Whether
# that is acceptable is a policy decision, so it is a setting:
#
#   config.block_privacy_relays = false  # default: relays are ALLOWED, even if a source lists them
#   config.block_privacy_relays = true   # relays are REFUSED, even if no source lists them
#
# Each domain covers its subdomains (addy.io hands out `alias@username.anonaddy.com`).
# Source: disposable/disposable `mailservices.json` (MIT), entries with
# "type": "forwarding", plus Apple's Hide My Email.
